About

Antik Mesele is an independent educational channel run by a single creator with a background in classical archaeology.

Researched

Every script is checked against museum, academic and news sources before production. Sources are listed in each video's description.

Original

Scenes are made in-house with 3D software. Only our own work or openly licensed (CC0 / CC-BY / public domain) material is used.

Published by us

Our own small publishing tool uploads each approved video to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It does not access anyone else's data. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
