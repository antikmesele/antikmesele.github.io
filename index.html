Privacy Policy: Antik Mesele

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Antik Mesele ("the app") is a small, single-owner publishing tool. It is used only by the channel owner to upload the owner's own short videos about archaeology and the ancient world to the owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram).

What the app accesses

  • The app connects to the channel owner's own accounts through each platform's official API and OAuth sign-in.
  • It requests only the permissions needed to upload and publish the owner's own videos and, where required, to confirm which account is connected (for example, the channel name).

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data of other users or viewers.
  • It does not read other people's accounts, messages, comments, or follower lists.
  • It does not use any data for advertising or profiling.

Data storage

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder that is not shared or published.
  • Videos, titles, and descriptions are the owner's own content and are sent only to the platform the owner chose.
  • No data is stored on a third-party server operated by the app.

Your choices

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the security settings of the connected account (for example, Google Account permissions, TikTok app permissions, Instagram or Meta app settings). After revocation the app can no longer publish.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact: antikmesele@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
